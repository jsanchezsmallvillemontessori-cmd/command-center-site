Command Center

A private desktop app that Jason Sanchez of Smallville Montessori built for his own use.

Command Center runs only on its owner's Mac. It brings his to-dos, projects, calendar, files, bills and important emails together in one place, so nothing gets missed.

It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for or download. Only the owner and a few test accounts he controls use it.

What it does with Google

With the owner's permission, it reads his Google Calendar and Gmail so his events, bill due dates and messages from people he chooses appear on his dashboard. Read the privacy policy for the details.
