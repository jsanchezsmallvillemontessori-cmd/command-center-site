Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

Command Center is a private app that runs only on its owner's Mac. It is used only by its owner, Jason Sanchez, and Google accounts he controls.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: to show events and to add or change events the owner asks for.
  • Gmail, read only: to find bills and due dates, and to show new mail from senders the owner chooses to watch. It never sends, deletes or changes email.

Where the data goes

  • Everything it keeps stays on the owner's Mac. Sign-in keys are kept in the Mac's Keychain.
  • It keeps only what the dashboard needs, such as a sender, a subject, a date and a bill's amount and due date. It does not keep copies of whole emails.
  • To read a bill's amount and due date, the text of that one email may be sent to Anthropic's Claude AI, using the owner's own Claude account. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or read by anyone other than the owner.

Google's Limited Use rules

Command Center's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Command Center's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
